# Code Of Conduct

LaunchDiff contributors are expected to keep the project welcoming, professional, and focused on careful technical work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Discuss tradeoffs with specific examples, tests, or documentation.
- Respect privacy and never post private Launch source, URLs, configuration, credentials, or customer data.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning language about individuals or groups.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask for focus.
- Attempting to bypass LaunchDiff's security or privacy boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's preferred contact channel.
